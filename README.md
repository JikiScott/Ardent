# The Vision:
There are national stats on how much of certain basic goods, intermediaries, and final products the economy has. There are three main elements to the economy: workers, firms, and the government. In order for a worker to work, they must either found or join a firm (company), both of which are regulated by the government. After workers join a company, when they use work commands (possibly with a minigame later) they give the worker income and gives the firm basic resources based on the firm's industrial sector.

Workers can then also spend money (or possibly in the future use capital goods) to convert basic resources to intermediary resources, and intermediates into products, which can be sold to an automated market. Investors can contribute to companies to help them hire more employees and buy whatever intermediaries (perhaps even capital goods) based on their final product production volume and sales.

Firms and workers have two main sources of cash income: chat income and work commands. Chat income just gives both firms and individuals small amounts of money per non-spam messaging (either limited pay out per minute or an automated filter.) 

Products are then sold to the government to generate the firm a profit and increase national statistics. For example, if a military production company produces a product and the government buys it, the 'military spending' national stat increases. Same with population for food, health for pharma companies, etc.
